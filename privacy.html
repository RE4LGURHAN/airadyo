GEDAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

GEDAI is a private application used only by the owner of the AI RADYO YouTube channel to upload, schedule and manage the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

GEDAI uses YouTube API Services. By using GEDAI you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the signed-in channel.
  • Video upload and editing permissions to publish and schedule the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
