GEDAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

GEDAI is a private application used only by the owner of the AI RADYO YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. It uploads, schedules and manages the channel's own videos, and it runs the channel's interactive live streams. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

GEDAI uses YouTube API Services. By using GEDAI, or by interacting with AI RADYO live streams, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID), to confirm the signed-in channel.
  • Video upload and editing permissions, to publish and schedule the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos.
  • Live chat of the channel's own live broadcasts: each viewer's message text, public display name and public profile picture URL.

How live chat data is used

During an AI RADYO live stream, viewers can type commands such as "çiz", "müzik" or "şarkı" to request an AI-generated image, instrumental track or short song.

The text of such a request and the viewer's display name are checked for safety and turned into the requested content. For this, the text and display name are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, an AI service. Anthropic processes them under its own privacy policy. They are not used for advertising.

The viewer's display name and profile picture are shown on the stream next to the content they requested. An AI voice may also say the display name on the stream.

Chat messages that are not commands are not processed.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens, analytics data and live-stream session logs are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.

Live chat data in session logs, and media generated for viewer requests, are deleted automatically after 30 days.

No data is sold, shared with third parties for their own purposes, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke GEDAI's access at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
